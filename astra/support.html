Frequently asked questions

How do I get my exact Rising sign?

Enter your birth time as precisely as you can. If you turn on “I don’t know my birth time”, Astra hides the Rising sign and houses because they change every few minutes.

Can I change my birth details?

Yes — open More → Edit my birth data.

Which house system does Astra use?

Placidus by default. You can switch to Whole Sign or Equal houses in More → House system. Near the poles Placidus is undefined, so Astra uses Equal houses automatically.

Why is my birth place not in the list?

Pick the nearest large city in the same time zone; the difference is usually a fraction of a degree. Tell us which city to add and we will include it in an update.

How do I delete my data?

Open More → Delete all my data, or delete the app. All data is stored only on your device.

Does Astra need an internet connection?

No. All calculations run on your iPhone.
