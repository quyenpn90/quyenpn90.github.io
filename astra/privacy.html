Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Astra (“the app”).

Information you enter

To calculate a birth chart, Astra asks for a name, birth date, birth time and birth place. You can also save the same details for other people to compare charts. This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal data. Astra has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous statistics (such as install counts and crash reports) if you have chosen to share them with app developers in your device settings.

Deleting your data

Open More → Delete all my data in the app, or delete the app. Both remove everything Astra has stored.

Children

Astra is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If a future version adds features that send data off your device (for example AI readings, analytics or ads), we will update this policy and describe them here before they are released.
