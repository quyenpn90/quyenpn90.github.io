Hidden Browser — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Hidden Browser ("the app") is a web browser for iPhone. This policy explains what data the app handles. In short: your browsing history, bookmarks, passcode and downloads stay on your device. We never upload them.

Data that stays on your device

  • History and bookmarks of regular tabs are stored only on your iPhone. You can delete them at any time in History or Settings › Clear browsing data.
  • Private tabs keep cookies and site data in memory only; nothing is saved to history and everything is erased when the tab is closed.
  • Downloaded files are saved in the app's folder on your iPhone (visible in the Files app).
  • App lock passcode is stored only as a one-way hash. Face ID / Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS; the app never receives your biometric data.
  • Cookies and website data are stored by Apple's WebKit on your device, as in any browser. Websites you visit have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Deny or change tracking permission at any time: iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Users in the EEA/UK can change ad consent in Settings › Ad privacy settings.
  • Remove ads entirely with the one-time "Remove ads" purchase.
  • Delete all app data by deleting the app.

Children

The app provides unrestricted web access and is not directed to children under 13.
