Hidden Browser

A fast, private web browser for iPhone

Hidden Browser is a lightweight web browser that keeps your browsing to yourself. Your history and bookmarks stay on your iPhone and are never uploaded.

  • Private tabs: no history, and cookies and site data are erased when you close them
  • App lock with a 4-digit passcode and Face ID or Touch ID
  • Blocks common ad and analytics trackers
  • Tabs, bookmarks, searchable history and top sites
  • Download files (PDF, images, documents) to the Files app

Common questions

What is saved in a private tab?
Nothing is added to your history, and the tab's cookies and site data are erased when you close it. Files you choose to download and bookmarks you add are kept.

I forgot my passcode.
The passcode is stored only on your iPhone and cannot be recovered. Delete and reinstall the app to reset it; this also deletes your history, bookmarks and downloads in the app.

Why can't I download a video from some sites?
Hidden Browser downloads regular files such as PDFs, images and documents. Downloading from video streaming and social media sites is not supported; you can still watch them in the browser.

How do I delete my browsing data?
Settings → Privacy & security → Clear browsing data.

What does "Remove Ads" do?
It is a one-time purchase that permanently removes banner and full-screen ads.
