Privacy Policy

App: Hueka · Last updated: 24/08/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hueka ("the app", "we") handles your information.

1. Information we collect directly

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, or contact details. Your settings and your progress in Challenge mode are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. The colours you mix are never sent anywhere — all mixing is computed on the device and the app works offline.

2. Advertising

Hueka is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain data, including:

  • Device advertising identifiers (such as the iOS IDFA or Google Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device information and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is used to deliver and measure ads. On iOS, we ask for your permission before any tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can decline. You can learn how Google uses this data here: google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Your choices

You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Within the app you can also open the ad privacy options from the Settings screen where available.

4. Children's privacy

Hueka is an educational app intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Third-party services

We use Google AdMob (advertising) and may use Google Firebase (app configuration and basic analytics). Their handling of data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy: google.com/privacy.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Please review this page periodically.
