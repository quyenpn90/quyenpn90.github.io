Privacy Policy

App: Dotto: Nonogram Puzzle · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dotto ("the app", "we") handles your information.

1. Information we collect directly

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email or contact details. Your settings, hints, solved puzzles and puzzle progress are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

2. Advertising

Dotto is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob and its mediation partners. To show and measure ads, these providers may collect:

  • Device advertising identifiers (the iOS IDFA, only if you allow tracking)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device information and ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

On iOS we ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking, and you can decline. Declining does not change the number of ads you see. Learn how Google uses this data: google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Analytics

We use Google Firebase (Analytics and Remote Config) to understand which puzzles are played and finished and to adjust app settings. This data is not linked to your identity.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

5. Your choices

You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Users in the EEA/UK can reopen ad consent choices from the app's Settings screen.

6. Children's privacy

Dotto is a puzzle game for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Third-party services

Google AdMob and Google Firebase are governed by Google's Privacy Policy: google.com/privacy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
