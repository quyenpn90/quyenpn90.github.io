Common questions

What does a hint do?
A hint reveals one full row or column of the current puzzle. New players get 3 hints; you can earn more by watching a short video or buy a hint pack.

Does "Remove Ads" remove the free-hint videos?
Remove Ads turns off banners and ads between puzzles. Free-hint videos are only shown when you choose to watch one.

Is my progress saved?
Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
